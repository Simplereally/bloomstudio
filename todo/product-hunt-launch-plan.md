# Product Hunt Launch Plan: Bloom Studio

## 1. Strategic Positioning
**Core Value Proposition:** Premium AI creation tools made accessible.
We are democratizing access to top-tier models (Flux, Veo) by undercutting the market significantly ($3/mo vs $20+ competitors).

**Key Narrative:** "Why pay $30/month for Midjourney when you can get the same quality for $3?"
*   **Simple:** No complex credits systems, just images.
*   **Affordable:** $3/month for 5,000 daily images.
*   **Powerful:** Flux, Veo, Grok Imagine.

## 2. Launch Checklist & Asset Guidelines

### A. Visual Assets (Crucial)
Detailed guidelines for 2024/2025 standards.
| Asset | Dimensions | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| **Thumbnail** | 240x240 px | **most important**. Static or GIF (animations on hover). <3MB. Keep it simple, logo + recognizable art. |
| **Gallery Images** | 1270x760 px | Upload 3-5 images. Show *results* (stunning AI images) and *UI* (clean interface). |
| **Header Image** | 1200x630 px | Optional but recommended for 2025 launches. |
| **Video** | 16:9 | Highly recommended. ~60 seconds. Show the workflow: Prompt -> Result. |

**Tip:** For AI tools, *show the output*. Your thumbnail should be a stunning generation that makes people click.

### B. Copy to Prepare
**Name:** Bloom Studio
**Tagline (Max 60 chars):**
*   *Option 1:* "Premium AI Art & Video. $3/month. No Commitments." (Direct)
*   *Option 2:* "The world's most accessible professional AI studio." (Grand)
*   *Option 3:* "Flux, Veo, and more. 24h Free Trial. Just $3/mo." (Feature-led)

**Topics:** Artificial Intelligence, Design Tools, User Experience, Video, SaaS.

**Description (250-300 words):**
*   **Hook:** High-end AI tools are too expensive ($20-100/mo).
*   **Solution:** Bloom Studio brings you the best open models (Flux, Veo) for a fraction of the cost.
*   **Features:**
    *   Text-to-Image (Flux) & Text-to-Video (Veo).
    *   Private generations.
    *   24-hour completely free trial (no card required).
    *   Simple interface.

## 3. Launch Timeline & Tactics

### Pre-Launch (Now - 1 Week Out)
*   [ ] **Create "Coming Soon" Page:** Capture emails if possible.
*   [ ] **engage:** Log in to PH daily. Upvote, comment on other launches. (New accounts with 0 activity get deprioritized).
*   [ ] **Social Tease:** Share "building in public" updates on Twitter/X, Discord.

### Launch Day (Target: Tues/Wed/Thurs @ 12:01 AM PST)
*   **Timing is Key:** Post exactly at 12:01 AM PST to get the full 24-hour exposure.
*   **The "Shill":**
    *   Twitter thread linking to PH page (use `?ref=producthunt` in URL).
    *   Discord announcement.
    *   LinkedIn post.
    *   *Do not* generally ask for "Upvotes" directly (PH frowns on this). Ask for "Support" or "Feedback".
*   **Engagement:** Reply to *every* comment on PH within minutes.

## 4. Specific "Bloom Studio" Advantages to Highlight
Based on your current codebase:
1.  **"Nano Banana Pro"**: The name is catchy. If this is a custom fine-tune or special model, highlight it.
2.  **24h Free Trial:** This is your biggest friction reducer. Make sure this is FRONT AND CENTER in the Description and top comment.
3.  **Video:** Many "cheap" generators only do images. Highlighting Video (Veo) at the $3 price point is a killer feature.
